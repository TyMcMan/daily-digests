Molt: A Scalable PyTorch-Native Training Framework for Agentic Reinforcement Learning

NVIDIA NeMo's ~9.2K-line framework composing Ray (placement/async queues), vLLM (rollout), and FSDP2 (training) into one disaggregated async loop for agentic RL. Scales to 1T-class MoE models and achieves statistical parity with Megatron-based stacks under a matched async protocol.

Why it matters: Keeps the full agentic RL pipeline researcher-legible from end to end — low iteration cost for algorithm changes that currently thread through multiple abstraction layers. Open-sourced at github.com/NVIDIA-NeMo/labs-molt.

The Regression Tax: Decomposing Why Skills Help and Hurt LLM Agents

Formally decomposes net skill effects into a positive "skill dividend" (improvement on the target task) and a negative "regression tax" (capability degradation on other tasks). Identifies structural factors driving regressions and offers concrete curation guidance for production skill libraries.

Why it matters: Explains why naively giving agents more tools often degrades overall performance — a systematic framework for deciding which skills belong in a production agent's kit.

Token Budget Saturation and Mechanistic Early Detection of Reasoning Non-Convergence in Chain-of-Thought Models

Discovers bimodal convergence in CoT reasoning models (tested on DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B): runs that converge within budget reach 90.3% AIME accuracy vs 6.6% for budget-exhausting runs. Introduces mechanistic early-detection signals for non-convergence before token exhaustion.

Why it matters: Early-exit detection of failing CoT traces is directly actionable for inference cost management — route non-converging generations to a retry/fallback branch before they exhaust the budget.

Frontier Language Models Struggle to Copy: Text Can Be Better Viewed in 2D

Shows frontier LLMs systematically fail at exact string copying due to 1D positional encoding bias that creates local-context shortcuts. Proposes 2D-RoPE (row + column token IDs) that turns copying into a fixed-offset retrieval; shallow Transformers with 2D-RoPE achieve perfect copying at lengths hundreds of times beyond training distribution.

Why it matters: Exact copying is a primitive operation that agents, code models, and document processors rely on — this reveals a structural gap and a targeted fix.

Systematic benchmark of MLLMs on scientific visualization tasks (charts, plots, diagrams). Gemini is the strongest model overall; open-source models remain below human baseline.
