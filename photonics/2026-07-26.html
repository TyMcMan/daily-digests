📄 Research & Labs

Monolithic Magneto-Optical Mach-Zehnder Isolator via Laser-Annealed Ce:YIG on Silicon

Demonstrates a monolithic on-chip optical isolator built by locally crystallising cerium-substituted yttrium iron garnet (Ce:YIG) inside micron-scale trenches on a silicon waveguide using 915 nm laser annealing — leaving surrounding CMOS circuitry intact. The fabricated device achieves 13.6 dB isolation at 1540 nm (20.4 dB insertion loss on first demonstration) and is claimed compatible with high-throughput wafer-scale manufacturing. Why it matters: On-chip optical isolation is a long-standing silicon photonics integration gap; a CMOS-backend-compatible process could enable co-packaged laser sources and low-reflection PIC layouts directly relevant to photonic compute and optical interconnect designs.

Photonics21 Welcomes Chips Act 2.0 Photonics Inclusion, Calls for Dedicated Partnershipcatch-up

Photonics21 formally welcomed the European Commission's proposal to explicitly include photonics and integrated photonics in Chips Act 2.0, and called for a ring-fenced budget delivered through a dedicated MoU-based Photonics Partnership under Horizon Europe and the European Competitiveness Fund. The statement covers the full stack: integrated PICs, biophotonics, free-space optical comms, quantum, and photonic computing. A Board of Stakeholders election (37 seats) is scheduled for October–November 2026. Why it matters: Explicit Chips Act 2.0 inclusion is a prerequisite for EU public funding to flow to PIC foundries and design tool developers; this statement sets the negotiating position ahead of the formal legislative process.
