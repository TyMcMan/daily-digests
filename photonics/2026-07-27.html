Innolume Selects Veeco Gen2000 MBE to Expand Quantum Dot Laser Productioncatch-up

Innolume GmbH (Dortmund) — a leading supplier of QD (InAs/GaAs) lasers for silicon photonics — has chosen Veeco's Gen2000 molecular-beam epitaxy system to scale production capacity. Why it matters: QD lasers are the emerging preferred light source for heterogeneous integration with silicon PICs in co-packaged optics modules; growing production capacity signals the CPO supply chain is maturing ahead of anticipated 2027 AI-infrastructure volume ramps.

Riber Launches ROSIE MBE Platform for Electro-Optic Silicon Photonicscatch-up

Riber (Bezons, France; Euronext: RIB) announced the ROSIE MBE system, optimised for depositing electro-optic oxides (e.g. BaTiO3) on silicon substrates at wafer scale. EO-on-silicon enables modulators with lower drive voltage and higher bandwidth than native silicon. Why it matters: BaTiO3-on-Si integration is a active route toward Vpi < 1V modulators for 1.6T+ coherent and intensity-modulated links; ROSIE positions Riber alongside II-VI/Coherent in the MBE tooling market for PIC fabs targeting next-generation modulator PDKs.

Credo Semiconductor Completes Acquisition of DustPhotonicscatch-up

Credo Semiconductor (NASDAQ: CRDO) has closed the acquisition of DustPhotonics, an Israeli silicon photonics startup developing 800G and 1.6T optical transceivers using monolithic silicon photonic integration. Why it matters: The deal extends Credo's SerDes and AEC product line into full photonic integration, accelerating their ability to compete in the AI-datacenter interconnect market where CPO and silicon photonic solutions are displacing pluggable optics above 1.6T. Watch for combined product roadmap announcements at OFC 2027.
