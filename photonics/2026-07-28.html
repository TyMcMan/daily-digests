📄 Research & Labs

Micro-electromechanical photonic integrated memristors

Zimmermann et al. present an analog-programmable photonic memristor built on MEMS cantilevers in a standard CMOS foundry, using low-loss SiN waveguides with all-electrical programming. The device provides multi-level, non-volatile optical phase storage interrogatable at the speed of light, enabling parallel data readout and energy-efficient MAC operations for optical neural networks. Why it matters: first fully CMOS-compatible PIC memristor — brings in-memory optical compute a step closer to wafer-scale manufacturing.

Photonic memristive devices for in-memory matrix operationcatch-up

Li, Chen, Su et al. survey performance trade-offs, fundamental constraints, and engineering bottlenecks of integrated optical memristors for large-scale matrix operations, covering phase-change, electro-optic, and MEMS device families. The review benchmarks energy per operation, endurance, and scalability against electronic SRAM/DRAM baselines. Why it matters: a timely reference for PIC designers evaluating non-volatile weight storage for on-chip inference — pairs directly with the MEMS preprint above.
